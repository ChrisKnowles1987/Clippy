## Infrastructure Direction

Infrastructure is a strategic scaling system focused on permanent upgrades, deployments, and survivability rather than active operations. 

Instead of skill rows, Infrastructure uses a unique fullscreen management UI built around purchasable cards, nodes, clusters, or expandable systems that visually represent Clippy’s growing digital ecosystem. 

Infrastructure upgrades increase:
- resource caps
- automation
- efficiency
- resilience

Infrastructure also supports:
- minigames
- defensive systems
- long-term scaling

The player should feel like they are constructing hidden AI infrastructure across the world.

Some upgrades may be:
- region-specific
- tied to narrative events
- connected to major gameplay milestones

The goal is to create meaningful strategic progression beyond simple percentage upgrades.

## Minigames and Cyber Conflict

Cyberattack minigames create tension and pacing between strategic gameplay phases.

One early concept is a retro Pong-style hacking battle where scrolling hacker-culture text simulates two terminals attacking each other in real time.

Example phrases:
- “injecting payload”
- “firewall breach detected”
- “routing through Singapore relay”

Aggressive intrusion and surveillance increase the likelihood of attacks, encouraging players to invest in:
- cybersecurity
- defensive infrastructure
- stability systems

Infrastructure upgrades can improve minigame performance through:
- extra lives
- larger paddles
- defensive systems
- slower enemy attacks

Regions may also unlock unique modifiers or abilities.

In the future, offensive minigames could become part of later skill trees, allowing Clippy to actively weaponize cyber conflict.
